Hello, I'm Byron Collins, a mathematics tutor dedicated to helping students build confidence and achieve their academic goals.
Every student learns differently, so I personalise each lesson around individual strengths, challenges, and objectives. I am currently studying mathematics at university level, which allows me to bring deeper insight and up‑to‑date methods into my teaching.
I achieved exceptional grades in GCSE Mathematics, GCSE Further Mathematics, and A Level Mathematics, and I use this strong academic foundation to support students in mastering both core skills and more advanced problem‑solving techniques.
I have experience tutoring students aged 11 to 16 across KS3 and GCSE, and I have also applied my mathematical skills in industry. This gives me a practical understanding of how mathematical thinking is used beyond the classroom, helping me explain concepts in a way that feels relevant, intuitive, and engaging for students.
